Press H anywhere to open the player. Press ? (question mark) any time to pause the audio and see the full list of keyboard shortcuts.

  • Keyboard: press H from anywhere on the site to expand and focus the player; the same key collapses it again once focus is inside.
  • Mouse: use any Play button, or the player bar fixed at the bottom of the page — click "Expand player" for the full controls.
  • Screen reader (Browse Mode): the player is a labeled region at the very end of every page, under a heading named "Player" — press your headings key until you hear it, or use the "Jump to player" link right after the skip link. Once you tab or arrow onto a control, your screen reader will switch to focus mode and the full key map applies.
